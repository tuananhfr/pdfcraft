PDF – ERPCons portable mode

While this file sits next to pdfcraft.exe, PDF – ERPCons keeps its settings, log files, crash-recovery
autosaves and new digital IDs in the ERPCons\PDF folder beside it, and writes nothing to
%APPDATA% or %LOCALAPPDATA%. Copy the whole folder to move PDF – ERPCons with its settings.

Delete this file to use the normal per-user folders (%APPDATA%\ERPCons\PDF and
%LOCALAPPDATA%\ERPCons\PDF) instead. If ERPCons\PDF can't be written (for example on a read-only
drive), PDF – ERPCons says so and uses the per-user folders instead.
